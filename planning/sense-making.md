## Methodology & Scoring Criteria

Environmental scanning covers all seven **STEEPLE** factors (**Social, Technological, Economic, Environmental, Political, Legal, Ethical**), backed by **3 verified, citable sources per factor (21 findings total)** drawn exclusively from peer-reviewed journals, scholarly books, intergovernmental reports, and enacted statutes/regulations.

Each key finding is scored independently from **1 (low)** to **5 (high)** without summing totals:

| Criterion | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Impact** | Could the key finding highly disrupt or reshape the proposed solution? |
| **Relevance** | How likely is the key finding directly tied to the proposed solution? |
| **Uncertainty** | Is there a high negative confidence on the direction of the key finding? |
| **Urgency** | Does delayed response pose significant risks? |

## Strategic Implications for `agora-ping` (v1.1 Alignment)

| Highest-Priority Signal | STEEPLE Factors | How `agora-ping` v1.1 Responds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offline Android trait capture + barcode/QR scanning + official numeric descriptors cut transcription errors and prep time (#4, #5, #6) | Technological | Sequential one-trait-per-screen Kotlin wizard, bundled `v1.0` core descriptor decks (`CAS`, `SWP`, `TAR`, `YAM`), `AP1` offline QR tags + scanner, and per-crop R/Weka/STAR CSV/JSON export with codebooks. |
| Climate-resilient root crops remain severely under-researched relative to cereals while agrobiodiversity erodes (#7, #8, #10–#12, #15) | Economic, Environmental, Political | Lowers the cost of multi-site characterization and aligns directly with PhilRootcrops (PD 1107) and ITPGRFA conservation goals through opt-in zone-pinned cultivar sharing. |
| Digital agriculture fails without simple interfaces, offline resilience, and institutional trust (#1–#3) | Social | Single-player-first offline utility (zero login required in the field), one-trait-per-screen flow, `NA` skip support, and zero financial bait. |
| Data Privacy Act (RA 10173 + IRR), Nagoya ABS, and farmer data sovereignty demand strict privacy and consent (#16–#21) | Legal, Ethical | Default `local_only` storage, explicit opt-in & revocable consent, coarse zone polygon generalization, zero per-user trails, zero-PII `AP1` QR tags, `k >= 5` regional aggregation thresholds, `30d`/`7d` draft retention limits, and private guideline hits with no public reputation scores. |
